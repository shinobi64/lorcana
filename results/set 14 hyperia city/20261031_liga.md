### Round 3

Last round pairing was with Annika from my own team. She brought the 🟢⚪️ Emerald Steel evasive build and won the dice roll. The first game was very close and she top decked the needed Elinor to win the game. In hindsight I should have challenged down her board instead of questing by myself. In the second game she stood no chance as I could clear the board early. In the last game it was flipped and she had the perfect line to clear my board, place Elinor and recur the Strength using Max Goof - no way to win this.
