# Wizard´s Well - Friday Evening Hyperia City Prerelease (Sealed)

Set 14 Hyperia City pre release event in Wizard´s Well. In total 6 showed up to play in a event with format Sealed. Tracking via https://tcg.ravensburgerplay.com/events/1086627.

## Wizard´s Well - Meta Report

### Spieler 6

All players on sealed decks.

## Tournament Rounds

My card pool was really good and had some synergies between ink drops and removal actions. In addition there was a good top end and a good number of evasive characters.

### Round 1

In round one I was paired with Anni. Her card pool was not so good, especially with regards to the top end. As well my evasives could quest out for a lot of lore before they could be removed.

Result after round 1: **1-0-0**

### Round 2

Second round was with Greta and I could there play quite fast, while Greta quested aggressive as well. In the end it was a very close finish in my favor.

Result after round 2: **2-0-0**

### Round 3

Last round was with Andre and his quite good pool. However I could bring down more evasives, especially in the early game which then ensured that I could gain the lore advantage to carry me to the win.

Result after round 3: **3-0-0**
