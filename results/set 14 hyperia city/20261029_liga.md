### Round 1

In round 1 I was paired against Nils with his 🟢🔴 Emerald Ruby Leviathan deck. This is scary as it kind of employs the same emerald control cards as my own deck but in addition has some solid ruby cards too. However it's even more combo dependent than my deck as it requires the Olaf and the Retro Evolution Device to enable the Leviathan.

### Round 2

In the second round I was paired with Manuel and his 🟡🟣 Amber Amethyst midrange deck. This is a rather good matchup but can be bad if the opponent can clear out the singers early on. In the first and third game I could get my songs chained up properly to control the board and then quest to the finish. In the second game Manuel could remove my singers with triple Isis and then a bodyguard to protect everything - I could not find a single Malicious Mean and Scary to enable the board with with Prince Philipp.

### Round 3

Last round of the evening was against Felix with a 🟢⚪️ Emerald Steel evasive deck. This is a scary matchup, as this deck can remove my singers early and the evasives are harder to combat. In the first game I could employ the Malicious Mean and Scary together with Wither a Flower combination, while in the second game I could not find anything to fight for the board. In the last game the Don Karnage together with Wither a Flower enabled double time the single sided board wipe.
