## Tournament Rounds

I decided to play an 🟡🔵 Amber Sapphire control version this time. The main advantage of the deck is to have control over the longer matchups and use the Cinderella effects for additional ramp. The new Minnie Mouse can recur cards from the inkwell and Moana allows inking from the discard. Challenges are wide boards as the main removal is direct spot removal and otherwise just the four Raging Storms.

### Round 1

In round one I was paired with Greta and her very aggressive 🟡🔵 Amber Sapphire aggro deck. This deck plays a lot of two questers and characters with ward which makes it hard to remove them with spot removal. In both games she got lucky with the Mike to bottom my Raging Storm, so I lost both games just shy of my own lore victory.
