## Tournament Rounds

I was quite ok with my sealed pool, it was lacking the top end and as well the number of evasive characters was pretty limited.

### Round 1

Round 1 pairing was with Annika, and her pool had a lot more top end and evasives than mine, so the game was over pretty quickly as soon as we approached the 5/ 6 ink mark.

Result after round 1: **0-1-0**

### Round 2

The game was close, but in the end the play experience helped to win with my limited pool.

Result after round 2: **1-1-0**

### Round 3

Final pairing was with Andre and his pool was as well way better than mine with regards to top end cards, especially the Don Karnage.

Result after round 3: **2-1-0**
