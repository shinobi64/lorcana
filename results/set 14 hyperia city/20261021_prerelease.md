# SouthWestTradingTeam - Monday Evening Hyperia City Prerelease (Sealed)

Hyperia City Set 14 pre release event in SouthWestTradingTeam. In total 10 showed up to play in a event with format Sealed. Tracking via https://tcg.ravensburgerplay.com/events/953577.

## SouthWestTradingTeam - Meta Report

### Spieler 10

All players on sealed decks.

## Tournament Rounds

My pool had some good cards, especially a good top end which helped a lot to win the games later in the rounds. I did not went too much up in the curve but kept it at 6 ink cost maximum.

### Round 1

In round one I was paired with Tim and it were three interesing games, in which I could win one game on the draw and then as well my game on the play. Finally I could bring out the Wasabi to control the board, as there is then really no way to quest anymore with a Wasabi out.

Result after round 1: **1-0-0**

### Round 2

In round two I had to play against Andre from our own team. My pool turned out way better than his pool, especially around the top end. Due to this I could win my both games on the play.

Result after round 2: **2-0-0**

### Round 3

Final round was with Patrick. He had a good pool with some very good but expensive top end cards. I could play a bit on the faster side with my quests and then have the Wasabi out again to gain the board control.

Result after round 3: **3-0-0**
