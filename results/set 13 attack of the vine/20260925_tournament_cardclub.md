### Top 8

In the top cut the first encounter was with Erik and his 🔵⚪️ Sapphire Steel midrange deck. This is a scary matchup as the deck plays a lot of very well statet characters and can deal quite some damage utilizing the actions and as well Merida. The deck lacks however the card draw to go wide. I could go fast and wide in the first game and then I could benefit from a playing mistake by Erik and a lucky Elinor topdeck to win it. In the second game then I could go wide and close with a double Broadway bodyguard play.
