### Round 1

Round 1 pairing was with Günter and his 🟢🔵 Emerald Sapphire Mickey deck. This deck is focused on bringing down the new combo shift card as early as possible to have a big body and a big quester. Then the deck plays all the big sapphire characters with ward to quest fast to victory. This strategy worked for Günter in the second game, while in game one and three I could go wide fast and use the Elinor triggers effectively to control the board.
