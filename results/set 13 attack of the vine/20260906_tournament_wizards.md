Round four pairing was as kind of expected the match against Anni with her 🔴🟣 Ruby Amethyst Evasive deck. I won the dice roll and could win my two games on the play as I could find there each time the way into Demona early. In the second game Anni found the evasive line and could bring out the Medallion Weights early to make her challenges better and generate card advantage too.
