### Round 2

Round 2 pairing was with Philipp and his 🟡🟢 Amber Emerald aggressive deck utilizing the Under the Sea and Elinor. In the first match I had a very slow start, while Philipp could establish double Elinor to make a quick lore gain and win. In the second game I was actually able to stabilize my board but I made a mistake not removing the Elinor with Worlds Greatest Criminal Mind and the support from my Lilo & Stitch so Philipp could gain too much advantage.

### Round 3

Last round was with Max and his 🟣⚪️ Amethyst Steel Bots based deck. This is a tough matchup as the Omnidroids are all very huge and most of them as well have Resist which is bad for Steel based removal actions. In addition the main target has to be the Syndrome as he enables a lot of the tempo plays with the droids. In the first game I had my early songs to control the board directly and then quest to victory. In the second game I was overwhelmed by the droids and had not answers in time. In the last game Max had a again a great start with Syndrome and two large droids, but was then missing the follow up pieces. I could stabilize and remove all these pesky droids and then utilize my Lilo and Stitch to quest and have positive trades at the same time for a close victory.
