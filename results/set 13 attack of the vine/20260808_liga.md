### Round 1

Round 1 pairing was with Philipp and his 🟡🟢 Amber Emerald aggressive deck. I know that he runs his list with focus on Under the Sea to specifically counter the aggressive decks like mine. In the first game I could get a good start, but he could counter this with the high willpower one drops followed by Dale to effectively trade down my deck. In the second game I could prevent one Under the Sea with Ursula, but he could find directly a second one using the Meilin Lee.

### Round 3

Last round was with Annika and her 🟡🟢 Amber Emerald Princess deck. The deck is nearly identical to mine but did not play the Under the Sea. In the first game I hold on to an Under the Sea but Annika found the Mowgli to discard it. I could then establish a Dale followed by two David bodyguards to get the board under the control. In the second game Annika started very good into Elinor but I could apply the same strategy Philipp used against me so bring down the high willpower one and two drops and then play Dale to trade the whole board down. From there on I was then fast to rebuild and with the Dale on board all challenges were favorable for me.
