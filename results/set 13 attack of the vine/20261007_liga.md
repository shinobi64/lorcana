# Card Club - Card Club - Lorcana Local

Another event in the Card Club. As Set 13 comes to a close we decided to give the Coconut format a try. Seven players showed up to play this interactive multiplayer format in a casual way.

## Tournament Rounds

We decided to break up into a group of four and a group of three and play three rounds in differnent combinations. In all three rounds we had a lot of fun with the interactions that are generated by playing lesser known cards and cards which bring in unexpected effects in a multiplayer environment. There was lots of laughter, cheers and time bound alliances.

Overall a format which closely resembles the well known Commander format from Magic and brought some older cards back to life.
